Health Protocol · 2026

If you want to fix low testosterone, here are 31 science-backed tips you could come up with

TL;DR
  • Nothing besides TRT/steroids will drastically increase levels.
  • Don't fall for fancy supplement labels.
  • The key is lifestyle changes, with some supplements as support.
  • Morning and evening rituals matter — timing your supplements correctly is half the work.
  • Sleep, diet, stress, and training compound over time. There are no shortcuts.

I've seen countless posts/advice on boosting testosterone. Let's be real — nothing besides TRT/steroids will drastically increase levels. Don't fall for fancy supplement labels. The key is lifestyle changes, with some supplements as support.

This is what you can do. This is backed by science.

Morning Ritual

01

Vitamin D3

Supports hormone function and bone health. Research has shown that Vitamin D3 supplementation can improve testosterone levels in men with low Vitamin D status.

Pilz, S., et al. (2011)

02

Tongkat Ali

Known for boosting libido and testosterone levels. Studies show it may improve libido and have an effect on testosterone.

Tambi, M. I. B. M., et al. (2012)

03

B-Vitamin Complex

Essential for energy metabolism and overall health. B-vitamins play a role in energy metabolism, with B6 and B12 supporting nervous system function.

04

Boron

Trace mineral that influences testosterone levels. Some studies suggest boron supplementation can increase testosterone.

Naghii, M. R., et al. (2011)

05

Maca Root

Adaptogen known for enhancing energy, stamina, and libido (not testosterone). Research indicates it doesn't significantly impact testosterone levels.

Gonzales, G. F., et al. (2002)

06

Creatine

Supports muscle growth and strength and may influence testosterone levels. Studies link creatine supplementation with increased muscle mass and slight test increases.

Volek, J. S., et al. (1997)

Evening Ritual — 60 min before sleep

07

Zinc

Essential for testosterone production and immune function. Studies indicate zinc supplementation can increase testosterone.

Prasad et al., 1996

08

Magnesium (Citrate or Glycinate)

Supports muscle and nerve function; aids relaxation and sleep. Magnesium has positive effects on muscle, nerve function, and sleep.

Cao et al., 2009

09

Omega-3 Fatty Acids

Anti-inflammatory effects + supports good sleep. Research suggests Omega-3s support sleep.

Hansen et al., 2014

Adjustments

10

Remove Sugar and Processed Foods

Sugar intake disrupts hormonal balance. A study linked high sugar intake to lower testosterone.

Maggio et al., 2013

11

Remove Seed Oils

Use butter or extra virgin olive oil. Trans fats from seed oils negatively impact health.

Mozaffarian et al., 2006

12

Say No to Alcohol

Excessive alcohol lowers testosterone.

Emanuele & Emanuele, 1998

13

Adios Tobacco and Drugs

These destroy overall health and hormone levels, including vaping.

14

Hydration

Drink plenty of water throughout the day. Aim for 2 liters.

15

Test Diet

Focus on whole foods like eggs, red meat, butter, fruits, and vegetables. A diet rich in protein aids muscle building and weight loss, which support higher testosterone levels. Aim for 2x body weight in grams (e.g., 80kg = 160g protein).

16

Raw or Fat Natural Milk & Coconut Water

Highly beneficial.

17

Coffee Intake

Limit to morning hours, but drink 90 min after waking to avoid disrupting sleep.

Lifestyle Fixes

18

Weight Lifting — 3-5 times a week

Boosts testosterone levels. Studies show resistance training enhances test production.

Ahtiainen et al., 2003

19

HIIT Training

Short, intense bursts of activity boost testosterone.

20

Bedroom Activities (Morning)

Sexual activity (not self-induced) can influence testosterone levels.

van Anders et al., 2011

21

Regular Movement

Avoid sitting for prolonged periods — don't be lazy!

22

Outdoor Activities

Sunlight exposure is crucial for Vitamin D synthesis.

Sleep is Essential

23

Quality Sleep

Invest in a comfortable bed and pillow — maintain a regular sleep schedule. Sleep is crucial for hormone regulation.

24

Sleep Tracking

Monitor sleep patterns for improvement. Devices like the Ultrahuman Ring help.

Avoid Xenoestrogens & Estrogen-Like Substances

25

Personal Care & Household Products

Found in some plastics, shampoos, deodorants, and toothpaste — these disrupt hormonal balance.

Darbre, 2006

Stress Management

26

Cold Showers

Finish each shower with 30–60 seconds of cold water. Be a man. Get used to it.

27

Ice Baths — 2-4 times a week

Boost resilience, recovery, general health, burns fat + boosts test. Cold exposure activates brown adipose tissue (BAT), which burns calories.

van der Lans et al., 2013

28

Mindfulness & Relaxation

Meditation helps manage stress by lowering cortisol, which destroys testosterone. If meditation isn't for you, go for walks in nature.

Fasting

29

Fasting Once a Week (24h) or Every Two Weeks (36h)

Research suggests fasting has various health benefits, including potential testosterone impacts.

30

Short-Term Fasting

Increases LH (hormone stimulating testosterone production).

Anderson et al., 1987

31

Weight Loss & Testosterone

Fasting can lead to reduced body fat, which indirectly raises test levels.

Ng Tang Fui et al., 2017
