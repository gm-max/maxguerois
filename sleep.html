3 months of tracking — 7 tips that lead me to high-quality sleep

I started with a sleep score of 82. Within 15 days of applying these changes, I hit 100. Here's exactly what moved the needle — and what didn't.

Tip 01

Cut caffeine — hard

I went from 3 cups of coffee a day down to 1, switching the rest to decaf. Caffeine has a half-life of ~6 hours — that afternoon espresso is still active at midnight.

Tip 02

Move every day

Minimum 11,000 steps a day, plus a workout. Daily movement is one of the strongest sleep regulators — not optional.

Tip 03

Three supplements that actually work

Tested a lot. These three made a real difference:

  • 250mg Magnesium
  • 2g Glycine
  • 30mg Apigenin
Tip 04

Control your last meal

Eat between 6–7 PM. Keep it under 500 calories, avoid heavy protein, and nothing for 3 hours before bed. Digestion competes with deep sleep.

Tip 05

Optimize your sleep environment

Cool, dark, quiet. Target temperature: 60–67°F (15–19°C). I use earplugs.

"The parameter with the most impact on my score was skin temperature."

Tip 06

Consistent bedtime — no exceptions

I go to bed at 10:30 PM, ±30 minutes. Consistency trains your circadian rhythm. Sleeping in on weekends breaks what you built all week.

Tip 07

A real wind-down routine

30 minutes before bed: no screens. Read or meditate. Signal to your nervous system that the day is over. It sounds soft. It works.
